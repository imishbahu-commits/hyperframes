## Accuracy controls

- The official September 16 FOMC statement reports a unanimous 0.25-point increase to 3.75%–4.00% and says inflation remains elevated.
- The official Federal Reserve H.15 release reports the 10-year Treasury constant-maturity yield at 5.18% on September 24, 2026.
- Mortgage language explicitly avoids claiming that mortgage rates move one-for-one with the federal-funds rate.
- No security recommendation, guaranteed outcome, fabricated lender quote, or personalized advice appears in the script.
